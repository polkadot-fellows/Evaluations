## Argument

This period I focused on Web3 Storage (Capacity): building an example application end-to-end to showcase and validate it, researching the provider node's database layer and hardening its CI.
At the same time, I continued improving Bulletin Chain and HOP, and did XCM hardening work across the SDK and the fellowship runtimes.

A big bulk of my time went to the example Photos dapp[\[1\]](https://github.com/paritytech/web3-storage/pull/152), an example application that showcases how a normal app uses Web3 Storage.
