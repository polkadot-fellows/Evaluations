## Argument

I am applying for fast promotion to Rank II under [Rule 13 of the JAM Implementer's Prize](https://jam.web3.foundation/rules).

I have been a developer of [jamduna](https://github.com/jam-duna/jamduna), an independent JAM implementation written in Go, since July 2024. Our team successfully completed Milestone 1, which was approved by the Fellowship through [Referendum #648](https://collectives.subsquare.io/fellowship/referenda/648).

Following my Fellowship interview on November 24, 2026, I was advised to apply for Rank II.

The jamduna repository is currently private for clean-room reasons, but access can be provided upon request in accordance with Rule 26 of the JAM Implementer's Prize.

### Contributions to jamduna

My main contributions to jamduna include:

- Implementation and maintenance of the JAM state transition function.
- Development of the PVM recompiler.
- Testing and interoperability with other JAM implementations.
- Maintenance of the client across different Graypaper versions.

### Other JAM-related contributions (Manifesto §6.3)

I have also contributed to the development and sharing of JAM test vectors through [coreplay](https://github.com/jam-duna/coreplay), formerly known as jamtestnet.

Our team published testing datasets and traces for Graypaper versions 0.6.1, 0.6.2, 0.6.4, 0.6.5, 0.6.7, 0.7.1 and 0.7.2, covering:

- Block import and state transition.
- Safrole and fallback.
- Assurances and guarantees.
- Ordered accumulation and refinement context.
- Work package bundles and work report derivation.
- Availability and erasure coding.

These datasets supported early cross-implementation testing and collaboration with other JAM teams, including Polkajam ([#71](https://github.com/jam-duna/coreplay/issues/71), [#139](https://github.com/jam-duna/coreplay/issues/139)).

### Continued contribution

I intend to continue contributing to the development and maintenance of jamduna and work towards the remaining JAM Prize milestones.
